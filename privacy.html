Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you paste a link, our server receives that link so it can find the media. Like most websites, our server also records your IP address in its logs. We use your IP address only to limit the number of requests per visitor and to stop abuse.

What we do not collect

We never ask for your Instagram username or password. We do not need an account, an email address or any personal details to use the tool.

Files and links

Photos and videos stream from Instagram servers to your device. We do not keep copies. The media link found for a post is kept in a temporary cache for about 10 minutes and then deleted.

Cookies and third parties

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. If you add analytics or advertising later, list those services here along with their cookies.
