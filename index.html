Instagram Downloader

Save Instagram videos, Reels, photos and stories to your phone or computer.

Instagram Downloader by GramFetch lets you save videos, Reels, photos, carousel posts, stories and profile pictures from public Instagram accounts. Copy the link of a post, paste it in the box above, and tap Download. It runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install and you never have to sign in to Instagram.

Instagram has a Save button, but it only adds a bookmark inside the app. If the creator deletes the post or makes the account private, that bookmark stops working. Saving the actual file keeps a copy on your device that you can watch offline, use in a mood board, or keep for your own records.

How to download from Instagram in 3 steps

  1. 1

    Copy the link

    Open the post in the Instagram app or website. Tap the three dots or the share icon and choose Copy link.

  2. 2

    Paste it here

    Come back to this page and tap Paste, or long press the box and paste the link yourself.

  3. 3

    Save the file

    Tap Download. A preview appears for every photo or video in the post. Tap the button under each one to save it.

Stories

Profile pictures

Works on every device

Android: open this page in Chrome, paste the link and tap Download. The file goes to your Downloads folder and shows up in your gallery app.

iPhone and iPad: use Safari on iOS 13 or later. After you tap Download, Safari saves the file to the Files app under Downloads. From there you can tap Share and choose Save Video or Save Image to put it in your Photos.

Windows and Mac: any modern browser works. Files go to your normal downloads folder.

Things to know

  • Only public accounts are supported. Private accounts are protected by Instagram and no link based tool can reach them.
  • We do not ask for your Instagram username or password. If any downloader asks for it, close that site.
  • Files are not compressed or changed. You get what Instagram serves.
  • Please respect creators. Download only content you own or have permission to use, and do not repost other people's work as your own.

Frequently asked questions

Is this Instagram Downloader free?

Yes. There is no charge, no sign up and no limit on file size. A light rate limit is used only to keep the service fast for everyone.

Do I need to log in to my Instagram account?

No. You only paste a public link. Never give your Instagram password to any downloader website.

Can I download from private accounts?

No. Private accounts are protected by Instagram, so only content from public accounts can be saved.

Where are downloaded files saved?

Files go to your browser's default download folder. On Android this is usually the Downloads folder. On iPhone, Safari saves them to the Files app under Downloads.

What quality will my video be?

You get the same file Instagram delivers to viewers. GramFetch does not compress or re-encode it.

Do you keep copies of the files?

No. Files stream from Instagram's servers to your device. We only keep the media link in a short cache for about 10 minutes to speed up repeat requests.

Why did my download fail?

The most common reasons are a private account, a deleted post, an expired story, or Instagram briefly limiting requests. Wait a minute and try again, or check that the link opens in Instagram.
